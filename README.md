# Capital World

A first-person 3D business and life simulator made with Unreal Engine 5.
Start with almost nothing in the sunny West Coast city of **Havenport**, rent an apartment, open a coffee shop, and grow a business empire through a chapter-based story.

**Status:** planning complete. The Unreal project hasn't been created yet.
